Coffee with IPD

國際處咖啡會

In the hope of helping parents better understand student learning objectives, Coffee with IPD aims to shed light on our program and provide related information for parents based on different grade levels and subjects.

為了幫助家長更好地了解學生的學習目標，國際處咖啡會旨在闡明我們的課程規劃，並且根據不同年級和科目為家長提供相關的信息。
